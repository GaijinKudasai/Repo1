Site Administrator Note

Gaijin (外人): A Japanese word that literally means "outside person" and is used to describe a foreigner or non-Japanese individual.

Tradecraft (諜報技術): Specialized skills, techniques, and methods used particularly in the practice of espionage and intelligence gathering.

Gaijin Tradecraft: The act of melding into Japanese society by using acculturation: mimicry, conformity and just being aware of what is considered rude in another society

Japanese Word of the Day

Kudasai — adverb, Please. Specifically used to make polite requests when asking for something tangible or requesting an action.

The term comes from the honorific verb kudasaru(下さる) which means "to give”
Examples:
Mizu wo kudasai (水をください) - Water, please.
Matte kudasai (待ってください) - Please wait.
